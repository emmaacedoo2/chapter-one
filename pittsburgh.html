BLACK, GOLD & HEART

STEELERS
TERRITORY 🏈

Founded in 1933, the Steelers built a tradition that stretches far beyond Pittsburgh. Six Super Bowl wins, a home at Acrisure Stadium, and a sea of waving Terrible Towels: this is Steelers Nation.

Broadcaster Myron Cope started the towel tradition in 1975. Bring the energy, wave it high, and become part of the story.

6SUPER BOWL
VICTORIES

Pittsburgh Penguins

The Penguins play at PPG Paints Arena. Their Stanley Cup wins in 1991 and 1992 were the start of a championship tradition that included another back-to-back run in 2016 and 2017.

Penguins history and hockey

Pittsburgh Pirates

PNC Park is their North Shore home. Bill Mazeroski’s walk-off home run won the 1960 World Series, and the Pirates won again in 1979 under the “We Are Family” banner.
